Designed to be Remembered

Athletic proportions and uncompromising M design language. Every line and every detail of the BMW M4 serves a purpose, optimizing aerodynamics, cooling, and visual impact.

Exterior Architecture

M Kidney Grille

Frameless design with horizontal double louvers for optimal cooling air requirement.

Laserlight

BMW Laserlight with selective beam for maximum visibility and striking light signature.

M Forged Wheels

Lightweight design reducing unsprung mass, available up to 20 inches.

Aerodynamics

The front apron features large air intakes, while the sculpted side sills and rear diffuser optimize airflow and increase downforce at high speeds.

M Carbon Roof

Made from carbon-fiber-reinforced plastic (CFRP), it lowers the vehicle's center of gravity and increases agility while adding a racing aesthetic.

Quad Exhaust

The M sports exhaust system features four prominent tailpipes, delivering an unmistakable, emotionally rich M sound.

Driver-Centric Cockpit

Premium materials, M Carbon bucket seats, and intuitive controls.
